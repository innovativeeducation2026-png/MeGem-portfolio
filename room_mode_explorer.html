README: Physics Model, Formulas & Real-World Caveats

1. 3D Eigenmode Equations

Rectangular enclosures support three classes of standing waves calculated via: f = (c / 2) · √((nx/L)² + (ny/W)² + (nz/H)²) where c = 343 m/s.

Axial modes bounce between two parallel faces (one non-zero index), tangential modes involve four faces (two non-zero indices, ~3 dB stronger energy than oblique), and oblique modes involve all six surfaces (three non-zero indices).

2. Practical Tuning & Real-World Caveats

What it shows: How axial, tangential, and oblique resonances cluster in low frequencies and how moving SUB and SEAT alters pressure peaks vs. nulls.

Real-world caveats: Assumes rigid, lossless walls. Real car cabins add sheet-metal flex, glass, seats, and cabin transfer gain; rooms add wall compliance, damping, and absorption.
